01

Create Your Account

An account is your persistent identity. It stores your character, settings, and progress across every session. You will use it to log in each time you return.

  • Choose a unique username.
  • Provide your email address.
  • Set a strong password.
  • No email verification is required — you can register and play immediately.

After registering you will be taken to your Dashboard, where you can create your character. Returning players should use the Log In link instead.

02

Create Your Character

Every account holds exactly one character. Your character is your avatar in the world — their name, race, and profession are permanent choices that define your entire journey.

  1. From your Dashboard, select Create Character to begin the character-creation wizard.
  2. Choose a character name. This is permanent and cannot be changed later.
  3. Select a race. Your race grants unique traits and determines your faction allegiance — Valroian or Mordrath. You do not choose a faction separately.
  4. Select a profession. Your profession determines your combat role and skill progression.
  5. Review your rolled base stats. You may reroll up to 3 times before locking in your final numbers.
  6. Confirm your choices on the final review screen. Once confirmed, these decisions are permanent.
Permanent choices: Character name, race, and profession cannot be changed after creation. Review everything carefully before you confirm.

Understanding a MUD

Rites of Passage is a MUD — a Multi-User Dungeon. If you have never played one before, here is what you need to know.

The game is text-driven. Instead of clicking on things, you interact with the world by typing commands into a prompt and pressing Enter. The server responds with text describing what happens.

When you first connect, you will see:

  • A room name — where you are standing right now.
  • A room description — prose that sets the scene.
  • Visible characters, creatures, and objects — anything else present in the room.
  • Exits — directions you can travel (north, south, etc.).
  • A command prompt — where you type.

Think of it as a living novel that you control. Every action you take is a sentence you type, and the world writes back.

{# ── 05 · ESSENTIAL FIRST COMMANDS ────────────────────────────── #}
05

Essential First Commands

Below are the commands every new player should know. Type each one at the prompt and press Enter.

Command What It Does
look Examine your surroundings — the room, its contents, and anyone present.
score Display your character sheet — stats, level, health, and more.
inventory See everything you are currently carrying.
equipment View the gear you have equipped — weapons, armour, and accessories.
who Show other players currently online.
help Open the in-game help system — your reference for every command.

{# ── 06 · YOUR FIRST FEW MINUTES ──────────────────────────────── #}
06

Your First Few Minutes

Once you are inside the game, here is a quick checklist to get oriented:

  1. Type look and read your starting location carefully — it tells you where you are and what exits are available.
  2. Type score to review your character sheet. Familiarise yourself with your stats, level, and health.
  3. Type inventory and equipment to see what you are carrying and wearing.
  4. Type who to see if other players are online.
  5. Type help whenever you are unsure what a command does or what commands are available.

Take your time. There is no rush — explore at your own pace. The world rewards curiosity.
